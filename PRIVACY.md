# SafeGuard privacy policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

SafeGuard blocks adult content and sites or keywords that you add. It checks visited URLs and page titles and descriptions and, on pages that appear likely to contain adult content, may classify page images using a model packaged with the extension. These checks run locally in Chrome.

SafeGuard stores your custom blocked sites and keywords, focus timer, and preferences in Chrome's extension sync storage. Chrome may sync this data to your other signed-in Chrome installations according to your Chrome sync settings. It stores downloaded blocklist domains, sync status, and block counters in local extension storage.

To update its community blocklist, SafeGuard downloads public files from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) and OISD (`nsfw.oisd.nl`), typically once per day or when you click **Sync Now**. Those services receive a normal download request, which can include your IP address and standard network metadata. SafeGuard does not attach visited URLs, browsing history, custom blocked entries, page content, or images to these requests.

SafeGuard does not sell data or use analytics or advertising trackers. It does not transmit browsing activity, page content, or image classifications to the developer. Removing the extension deletes its local extension data from that Chrome profile, subject to Chrome's own sync and backup behavior.

For privacy questions, use the [repository's issue tracker](https://github.com/RealYusufIsmail/safeguard-extension/issues).
